# Image credits

Every image under `public/images/facilities/` except `cricket-grounds.webp` is
openly-licensed stock photography sourced from Wikimedia Commons. They show the
sport, **not** Red Ball Sports Arena, and the cards on `/red-ball` do not say so.

`cricket-grounds.webp`, the venture photographs and the player photographs were
supplied by the site owner and are not covered by this table.

## Attribution still owed

Licences in use: CC BY 2.0, CC BY-SA 2.0, CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, Public domain.

CC BY and CC BY-SA both **require** attribution visible to the reader. A file in
the repository does not satisfy that - the credit has to be reachable from the
page carrying the image, normally a `/credits` route linked from the footer.
That page does not exist yet, so the site is not currently compliant with these
licences. Build it, drop the stock images, or replace them with photographs of
the real facilities.

CC BY-SA additionally carries a share-alike term. It binds adaptations of the
image itself, not the site around it; cropping and re-encoding for the cards
counts as adaptation, so the adapted files stay under the same licence.
